Methodology

One value chain: market prices in → validated strength ratings out. Closing Asian-handicap and over/under odds are inverted into goal expectations (λ) for each fixture; a λ-space ridge then fits the team attack and defence strengths those prices imply. A team’s rating is Att + Def, in log-goals.

What sets this apart from a plain rating system is the discipline: every modeling claim must pass a pre-registered, out-of-sample gate — frozen before the results exist — before it graduates. The within-league team ratings passed Gate T (they beat Club Elo out of sample). The cross-league euro-linked scale behind the global ladder is still beta: its gate, Gate E, is pre-registered and scores on 2026-27 euro results.
