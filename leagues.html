Season projection beta

Where each club is likely to finish, from a Monte Carlo simulation of every remaining fixture on the market-implied ratings — the shaded strip is the full finishing-position distribution, darker = more likely; Rating is the club’s strength relative to this league’s average and xPos where it is expected to finish. It moves when a club moves relative to its own league: how high a whole division sits is set by its European ties rather than by anything domestic, so a Champions League board landing can take every club in a league down together without changing one thing about the table below. Once a league is underway it gets two views, and the buttons above each table switch between them: League table is what has actually happened (played, won, drawn, lost, goals, points) and Projection is what the market implies from here. Each is ordered by its own answer, so # is where the club is projected to finish in one and where it sits today in the other. Each number carries its trajectory this season beside it, drawn on the same scale as the number itself — hover a rating’s for the attack and defence behind it and where the club sits on the global ladder, and an xPos’s for where the season’s first projection had it. Both lines cover the same weeks, and both rise when the club is doing better. Experimental and not yet independently validated (see methodology); before the season starts this is the ratings prior over the full schedule. For the strength ladder see the global ladder.
