Season projection beta

Where each club is likely to finish, from a Monte Carlo simulation of every remaining fixture on the market-implied ratings — the shaded strip is the full finishing-position distribution, darker = more likely; Rating is the club’s attack + defence strength. Once a league is underway it gets two views, and the buttons above each table switch between them: League table is what has actually happened (played, won, drawn, lost, goals, points) and Projection is what the market implies from here. Both are ordered by the real table, so # is where a club sits today in either — and in the projection Proj is where it is expected to finish, with the move that implies from where it is. The outlined cell on the strip marks its position today, so the shading either side of it is what the market expects to change. Δ is the club’s move in projected finish since yesterday’s projection. Experimental and not yet independently validated (see methodology); before the season starts this is the ratings prior over the full schedule. For the strength ladder see the global ladder.
