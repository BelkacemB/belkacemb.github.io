Where each club is likely to finish, from a Monte Carlo simulation of every remaining fixture on the market-implied ratings — the shaded strip is the full finishing-position distribution, darker = more likely; Rating is the club’s attack + defence strength. Once a league is underway the rows are ordered by its real table: # is where a club sits today and MP, Pts, GF, GA are the season so far (beside the Att and Def components the Rating sums), while the model’s answer moves into Proj — the projected finish, and the move that implies from where they are. The outlined cell on the strip marks their position today, so the shading either side of it is what the market expects to change. Click xPos to sort by the projection, Pts to come back. Δ is the club’s move in projected finish since yesterday’s projection. Experimental and not yet independently validated (see methodology); before the season starts this is the ratings prior over the full schedule. For the strength ladder see the global ladder.
