Ten squad valuations the betting market disagrees with

August 18, 2026

Your values are crowd-sourced, and each league gets a full pass about twice a season. Between passes, nothing independent tells you which of ~130 squads has moved.

Betting markets do. Every match is priced by people who lose money when the price is wrong, and it moves the day something changes. That makes closing odds a strength estimate with skin in the game — one that never looks at a transfer fee.

We read those odds daily and lined the result up against your values. Osasuna is listed at €71m; the market prices that squad like a €184m one. Alavés, Lens, NEC Nijmegen and Groningen sit in the same place. Five clubs run the other way.

This is not a better valuation. It is a queue.

Where the number comes from

Bayes FC reads closing handicap and totals odds, turns them into expected goals per match, and fits an attack and defence rating for each club. Strength is attack + defence: one scale, seven leagues, updated daily.

No valuation data goes in.

The picture

Stronger squads are worth more. The ten labelled clubs sit farthest off the line.

Leagues sit at different heights — Serie A about +40% above it, La Liga about −25% below. Each club below is an outlier inside its own league too. Hover a dot to see its league.

It is not age

A young squad can be worth more than its results. So we added squad age as a second term.

It barely moves. R² goes from 0.871 to 0.875, about −6% per extra year at equal strength. Every gap below survives it.

Fiorentina looks like a lag: results fell, the value did not. Parma and Frankfurt sell well, so a premium is expected — but not +267% and +106%.

What it is for

A queue, ranked every day by how far the two numbers sit apart.

And a trend: a squad whose strength has climbed for a month while its published value held flat is a staleness alarm.

Limits

  • Value is not strength. Contracts, one marquee asset, an academy: a team rating cannot see them. These are clubs to check, not verdicts.
  • One snapshot. Strength 2026-08-16, values 2026-08-11. The trend over weeks is sturdier.
  • Thin-record and promoted clubs are held out. Their ratings are too noisy.
  • Age is a team mean. Minutes-weighted would be sharper.
  • The line carries a league offset. Read each gap against its league as well.

Happy to run this against your live values and compare notes. No strings.
