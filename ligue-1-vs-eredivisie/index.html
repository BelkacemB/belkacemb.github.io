    Is Ligue 1 stronger than the Eredivisie?
    Level is the average rating of each league's clubs on the global scale (attack + defence, from closing betting-market prices). The goal ratio is what the gap means on the pitch: goals an average club of the stronger league scores for each one it concedes to an average club of the other, on neutral ground. The record is every Champions League, Europa League and Conference League meeting between the two leagues' clubs — their best sides, over seasons the ratings have moved past, so it is history, not the measurement.

    As of 28 October 2026, Ligue 1 is stronger than the Eredivisie: an average Ligue 1 club would score 1.68 goals for every one it concedes against an average Eredivisie club on neutral ground. In European competition since 2023-24, Ligue 1 clubs have won 7, drawn 4 and lost 1 of 12 meetings with Eredivisie clubs.
